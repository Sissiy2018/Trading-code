- Earnings Per Share - Smart Estimate: The SmartEstimate is an indicator of future earnings that improves upon the accuracy of the mean estimate by placing a higher weight on recent forecasts and on top-rated analysts. Earnings Per Share is defined as the EPs that the contributing analyst considers to be that with which to value a security. This figure may include orl exclude certain items depending on the contributing analyst's specific model.
